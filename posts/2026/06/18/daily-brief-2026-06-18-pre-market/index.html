Today’s Takeaway

The market isn’t giving us a clean signal today, but it is telling us to exercise patience. The rally sparked by the US-Iran news might prove fleeting unless supported by more robust earnings and clear signs of easing inflationary pressures—and persistent treasury yields are not going away anytime soon. Buying the dip is a strategy that may work, but don’t confuse temporary volatility with long term strength.
