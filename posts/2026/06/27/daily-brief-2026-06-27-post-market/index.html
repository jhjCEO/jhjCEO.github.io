Key Stories: AI, Iran, and the Flight to Safety

The anxieties surrounding AI momentum clearly took center stage today. While ServiceNow, Salesforce, and others in the software space caught a bid on signs that the threat from OpenAI might be weakening—a welcome development for Oracle, which had underperformed Today's Voca underperformed[ˌʌn.dər.pərˈfɔːrm]Energy names underperformed as oil prices fell. This question is increasingly weighing on investors’ minds as valuations come under pressure. The SpaceX bond deal further highlights this uneasiness; while $25 billion is a significant capital raise, it also signals an investor pump the brakes on the AI frenzy – demanding more due diligence and recognizing that even visionary companies can stumble.

The escalating tensions with Iran injected a new layer of risk into the market equation. The U.S. strike in response to the attack on a cargo ship sent crude oil futures higher, initially. While oil retreated slightly after the initial spike, it remains elevated reflecting broader concerns about regional stability and potential disruptions to global energy supplies. GameStop’s surprisingly robust EBITDA guidance offered a brief detour into retail enthusiasm, but even that couldn’t completely offset the heavier sentiment elsewhere. The war-risk premiums in the shipping-insurance market are worth watching; if they begin to widen again following today’s actions, it would be another sign of increased risk aversion.

Finally, the shift toward healthcare stocks deserves attention. This isn’t merely a tactical move; it represents a fundamental reassessment of which sectors offer sustainable value and reliable earnings growth. AbbVie’s positive news regarding its Skyrizi pediatric indication reinforced this trend, further solidifying healthcare’s position as a safe haven in an uncertain market environment. Chevron considering more deals to power data centers also highlighted the growing synergy between energy and technology but was overshadowed by the broader risk-off mood.

Today’s Takeaway: Expect Volatility
#

The market is telling us that the AI narrative, while still powerful, is no longer a free pass for stratospheric valuations. Combined with escalating geopolitical risks—particularly concerning Iran and its potential impact on global energy markets —we can expect increased volatility in the weeks to come. The strong performance of healthcare stocks suggests investors are preparing for a period of heightened risk aversion and may be seeking more resilient sources of earnings growth.
