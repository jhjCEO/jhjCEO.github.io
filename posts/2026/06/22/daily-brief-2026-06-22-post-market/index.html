Key Stories:

Micron’s Blockbuster Earnings: The New S&P 500 Engine? Micron’s results undeniably stole the show. A nearly 1,000% profit growth is not just impressive; it’s a seismic event that’s beginning to move the needle for the entire market. The sheer magnitude of this rebound suggests we’re past the worst of the memory chip downturn and into an era where demand for AI infrastructure – data center builds in particular – is driving substantial, high-margin growth. While some may worry about valuation froth as Micron continues to soar, investors should acknowledge that this isn’t a purely speculative play anymore. It’s indicative of a broader trend towards semiconductor leadership which increasingly defines the investment landscape. Keep a close eye on upcoming earnings reports from other chipmakers – this is where we see if Micron’s performance is an anomaly or a harbinger of things to come.

The Warsh Factor: Baked In, But Not Forgotten: Kevin Warsh’s remarks regarding potential further rate hikes added a layer of cautiousness today. While the market has largely priced in several quarter-point increases over the next year, the prospect of a more hawkish Fed chair always carries some risk. However, the tepid reaction suggests that investors have become adept at navigating this cycle—they’ve been on this rollercoaster before. It’s also a reflection of their confidence in the economy’s underlying strength and willingness to tolerate higher rates for now, especially as long as the earnings picture remains robust. The real risk lies not in rate hikes themselves but in how they interact with an already stressed yield curve - any further widening could signal deeper economic concerns that investors might find harder to ignore.

Geopolitical Storm Clouds: Shipping Slows, Tensions Mount: It’s impossible to completely ring-fence the market from global turmoil. The renewed disruptions to shipping via Iran’s shutdown of the Strait of Hormuz are a serious concern - adding inflationary pressures at a time when central banks are trying to tamp down price gains. Combined with the ongoing crisis in Bolivia, and whispers of instability elsewhere—the world feels increasingly precarious. While markets often demonstrate remarkable capacity for resilience in the face of geopolitical events, these developments serve as a stark reminder that complacency can be dangerous. Investors should keep an eye on how swiftly these disruptions resolve themselves – persistent bottlenecks could erode investor confidence.

Today’s Takeaway:

The market is telling us this isn’t a bull market based on blind optimism; it’s built on calculated bets in specific sectors—primarily those benefiting from AI—and the willingness to accept some volatility along the way. Don’t chase performance blindly, but understand why certain stocks are soaring and how they fit within the broader narrative of growth and disruption.
