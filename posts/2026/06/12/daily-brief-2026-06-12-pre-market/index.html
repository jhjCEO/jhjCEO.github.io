Key Stories

Iran Deal & Oil Prices: The overnight drop in oil prices is the biggest story, without question. The prospect of increased Iranian oil supply has immediately knocked down crude inventories, catching those who were long in oil off guard. This is a relief rally, and the potential for a renewed leg higher remains contingent on the deal’s final terms and its impact on broader geopolitical stability. Don’t be surprised to see some profit-taking as the reality of the situation settles in. However, it’s crucial to remember the cautionary tales of overhyped IPOs past. As one investor rightly pointed out, a dip isn’t a blip for retirees. Today’s Takeaway
#

Don’t be lulled into a false sense of security by today’s rally. The Iran deal, while positive in the short term, introduces new geopolitical risks, and the SpaceX IPO, while exciting, is a high-stakes gamble.
