3. Hybrids Surge – EVs Fade? The breakout star of today wasn’t tech or AI but hybrids. Demand for electric vehicles has clearly cooled down as consumers face rising prices and range anxiety, while hybrid technology provides the function and price point that resonates with consumers right now. This trend speaks volumes about the shifting consumer mindset and a necessary correction in EV valuations—a reminder that disruptive technology doesn’t always follow a straight line. Investors should consider rotating away from heavily-valued pure-play EV stocks toward companies benefiting from this hybrid resurgence, particularly those involved in AI infrastructure supporting autonomous driving advancements within hybrid models.

4. Momentum’s Troubled Waters: One strategist is warning of a violent unwind for momentum trades this month—and the rumblings started today. The market’s recent run-up has been predicated on chasing the highest-growth, most-hyped stocks regardless of fundamentals. This strategy has worked wonders in the short term, but it’s inherently vulnerable to corrections during periods of uncertainty and a flight to safety. July has historically been a tough month for momentum strategies. Now with rate-cut optimism possibly waning, and the AI sector looking overvalued, it’s time for investors to assess their holdings, tighten stops, and be prepared for an abrupt shift in market direction.

Today’s Takeaway:

The relentless bull run is showing its first signs of fatigue. While a full-blown correction isn’t inevitable, the market is signaling that excess optimism has taken hold. Prudence over exuberance – now is the time to favor quality and resilience over chasing fleeting momentum and reprice exposure accordingly.
