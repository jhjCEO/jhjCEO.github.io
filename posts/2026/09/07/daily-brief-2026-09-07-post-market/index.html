Geopolitical Tensions and the Flight from Safety: The situation in Iran predictably sent oil prices higher, but the market didn’t panic. This reflects a wider, more concerning trend: the U.S. losing its safe-haven status. The Netherlands and France’s decisions to pull gold out of New York vaults is a significant development – a reflection of waning confidence in the dollar’s and U.S. Treasury’s ability to act as a global repository of value. This is compounded by anxieties surrounding upcoming elections and the perception of political instability, leading some global central banks to explore alternatives. The long-term implications for the dollar and U.S. Treasuries are profound and warrant careful scrutiny.
